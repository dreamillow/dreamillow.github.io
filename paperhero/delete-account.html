Paper Hero: Idle RPG — Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Paper Hero: Idle RPG, developed by Dreamillow.

Delete in the game

  1. Open the game and tap the Menu (settings) button at the bottom left.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all of your game data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the game, email dreamillowgames@gmail.com with the subject [Paper Hero] Account deletion. Include your UID (shown in Settings > Account) or the Google / Apple account you linked. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and the Google / Apple sign-in link
  • All game progress saved in the cloud
  • Game progress saved on the device you delete from

What may be kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies, not by us.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports (Firebase) that cannot identify you may be kept for up to 14 months.
  • Advertising partners keep data under their own policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
